01 / CAPTURE

Start with a document

Create a scan using your camera, or import images from your photo library. Add multiple pages to keep a document together.

02 / REFINE

Make every page count

Rotate and reorder pages, apply grayscale or high-contrast filters, and add typed notes. Use text recognition to extract and copy text.

03 / EXPORT

Choose your PDF size

Open PDF export and choose Compact, Balanced, or High quality. Compact reduces image resolution and file size; High retains more detail.

04 / KEEP

Save a separate copy

Preview your PDF, then use Share to save it to Files or send it to another app. Export important documents before uninstalling PaperScan.

Common questions

The camera will not open. What should I do?

Check that camera access is enabled for PaperScan in iOS Settings. Try reopening the app. You can also import existing photos.

How can I improve text recognition?

Use a well-lit, sharp image with straight, unobstructed text. Avoid glare and shadows. Recognition accuracy varies by language, typeface, and image quality; review extracted text before using it.

Why is my PDF large?

Try Compact export quality, or remove unnecessary pages. A document with many high-resolution images will usually be larger. Check that small text remains readable after reducing quality.

Where are my documents stored?

Documents are stored in the app on your device. PaperScan does not provide its own cloud sync. Device backups and exported copies depend on your Apple and storage settings.

How do I delete my documents?

Delete a document from your PaperScan library to remove its stored pages from the app. Exported copies and backups must be managed separately in the services or locations where you saved them.
