Privacy policy

PaperScan for iOS · Effective November 14, 2026

Who operates PaperScan

PaperScan is provided by Mohamed Zayed. For privacy questions, contact paperscan.support@gmail.com.

Documents and on-device processing

PaperScan stores scanned and imported images, recognized text, document titles, and typed notes in its local app storage. Scanning, image processing, text recognition, and PDF generation run on your device. PaperScan does not upload this content to a developer-operated server.

The current iOS app does not require an account and does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect your document content through normal use of the app.

Permissions

Camera permission is used to scan documents. The system photo picker allows you to select images to import. You can manage camera permission through iOS Settings.

Sharing, backups, and Apple services

When you export or share a PDF, you choose the destination. That destination may store or transmit the file under its own privacy policy. PaperScan has no control over copies saved in other apps or services.

App data may be included in device or iCloud backups depending on your settings. PaperScan does not implement a separate cloud-sync service. Apple may process diagnostic or TestFlight feedback information according to your settings and Apple's policies.

Email support

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues. Support email is handled through Gmail and is also subject to Google's processing practices.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve and follow up on the request, or to meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. Please avoid sending sensitive documents.

Deleting your information

You can delete documents inside the app. Removing the app deletes its local app data, subject to the behavior of iOS. Exported files and backups may remain and need to be deleted separately. We cannot access or remotely delete documents stored only on your device.

This support website

This website does not include analytics scripts, advertising, or a document upload form. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and processes hosting data under its privacy statement. Clicking an email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically.

Policy updates

We will update this page if the app's data practices change and revise the effective date above. Contact us at paperscan.support@gmail.com with questions or requests about information you have sent to us.
